# Night Shift Motion Showcase

## Format

- Canvas: 720 x 1280
- Duration: 7 seconds
- Delivery: H.264 MP4, vertical social ad
- Theme: A midnight creator session converges into Tiger Studio

## Story Beats

1. `0.00-1.20` - A neon city and moving car establish speed. `NIGHT SHIFT`
   enters from opposite sides.
2. `1.20-2.20` - The editor workspace slams in as a tilted image card.
3. `1.70-2.80` - The sound console answers from the opposite direction.
4. `2.20-4.90` - The virtual singer rises into the foreground. The three
   image cards continue independent parallax, rotation, and scale motion.
5. `4.90-5.60` - The collage compresses and the background darkens.
6. `5.60-7.00` - The layout resolves to `TIGER STUDIO` and
   `CREATE / ANIMATE / BROADCAST`.

## Layer Intent

- `Night City Car`: continuous background push-in.
- `Editor Workspace`: right-to-left impact with overshoot.
- `Sound Console`: left-to-right impact with counter-rotation.
- `Virtual Singer`: foreground rise, overshoot, and controlled drift.
- `Accent Bars`: short timing markers supporting the three impacts.
- `End Card`: dark veil, title, divider, and product promise.

## Source Assets

The four image cards were generated for this showcase with the built-in image
generation tool. They are kept as durable sample assets so the composition can
be regenerated without relying on `debugCapture`.
